# Before the camp — RVU Camp
By: Ramez Medhat · rv-u.tech

A preparation checklist, not an admission test. Work on missing items or discuss them with me during the interview.

## Foundations
- [ ] Explain the difference between a request and a response.
- [ ] Understand URLs, methods, headers, bodies and status codes.
- [ ] Understand accounts, sessions, authentication and authorization.
- [ ] Intercept a request with Burp Suite in a training environment.
- [ ] Navigate files and folders and run a simple terminal command.

## Setup
- [ ] Computer, charger and stable internet.
- [ ] Burp Suite and a testing browser ready.
- [ ] Working Discord access and clear audio.
- [ ] Space for notes, recordings and training files when needed.
- [ ] Screen sharing tested before the first session.
- [ ] Read RVU Commerce setup instructions in its repository and follow the version used in the camp.

## Time
- [ ] Review the Monday and Thursday schedule: 7:30–10:30 PM, Cairo time.
- [ ] Set aside 5–7 practice hours weekly, in addition to live sessions.
- [ ] Collect questions for the private review every two weeks.

## Document an attempt
Record: What did I expect? What did I change? What actually happened? What evidence supports it? What remains unclear? What should I try next?

## Before the interview
Prepare a short description of your level, one attempt where you got stuck, your goal and your available time. The purpose is to understand your starting point, not memorize answers.
