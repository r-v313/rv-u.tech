03.B / MythBusters Matrix

Bug Bounty MythBusters Matrix

Deconstructing the 4 biggest misconceptions holding hunters back before they even start.

❌ MYTH 01
"Automated Scanners & AI Already Found All The Bugs"
🟢 REALITY
Scanners and AI lack human contextual reasoning. They cannot parse business workflows, state manipulation, nested authorization, or race condition sync attacks.
❌ MYTH 02
"You Must Be A C/Assembly Master To Hunt Bounties"
🟢 REALITY
Over 80% of top critical bounties paid out on HackerOne stem from Web API authZ flaws, IDOR, GraphQL, and HTTP logic bypasses — not binary exploitation.
❌ MYTH 03
"Public Scopes Are Too Crowded With 50,000 Hunters"
🟢 REALITY
95% of hunters only run default automated scanners on main domain targets. Through deep API mapping and JS parsing, you hunt on assets nobody else looks at.
❌ MYTH 04
"Bug Bounty Is Just A Random Game Of Pure Luck"
🟢 REALITY
Hunting is a structured methodology. Following a rigorous request-flow audit process yields repeatable, triaged findings on real targets.
