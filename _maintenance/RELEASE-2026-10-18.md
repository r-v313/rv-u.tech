## Security and operating limits

This review covered the public static website and its client-side application flow. It was not an audit of GitHub infrastructure, Formspree internals, YouTube, analytics providers or the separately hosted training-lab backend.

Application-service responses were mocked locally to verify success, failure, pending and duplicate states. No actual application, WhatsApp message or payment was submitted. Delivery to the owner's Formspree inbox was not verified.

The HTML uses a restrictive CSP with explicit service origins, no unsafe-inline or unsafe-eval, blocked inline event handlers, no objects and no base URL changes. CSP is delivered in HTML because of the current hosting setup. Response checks did not show HSTS or X-Content-Type-Options headers. These are host-level hardening opportunities, not demonstrated exploits; adding them requires configurable response headers at the hosting or CDN layer.

No live load test was performed. Static assets are cached by the host; videos are loaded on demand from YouTube. Actual concurrent-visitor capacity and Formspree quotas were not measured. No claim of unlimited capacity or complete absence of vulnerabilities is made.

Student review cards remain explicitly labelled illustrative samples. Genuine testimonials have not yet been supplied.
